# The dog card deck builder: idea

Exploratory, not designed. Moved here from its `docs/next-sessions.md` entry on 2026-10-26. Grows from
the dog fight in `docs/nosing-around.md` ("Next build: the dog"); best started once its card, grid and
operator modules exist.

## The question

Should the dog fight grow into a real deck builder (Slay the Spire style)?

## Owner's seed idea

- Start with one tarot suit (ace to king, 14 cards) plus a small operator deck.
- After each fight, pick one of three cards from other suits, or remove one.
- Suits only mean something once they mix, so cards from other suits could carry small effects, e.g.
  cups a life back on an exact hit, swords +1 damage on a double, pentacles draw a card, wands any
  operator.
- The major arcana (0–21) are rare rewards with bigger effects (the Fool as a wild card).
- A run is a series of stronger dogs, so it needs saved progress between fights.

## To settle
